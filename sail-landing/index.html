FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

What types of chronic pain does Sail help with?

+

Sail is designed to help with various types of chronic pain including back pain, fibromyalgia, arthritis, migraines, and other persistent pain conditions. Our evidence-based approach adapts to your specific pain pattern.

Is Sail a replacement for my doctor?

+

No, Sail complements your existing healthcare. We provide self-management tools and techniques that work alongside medical treatment. Always consult with your healthcare provider about your pain management plan.

How quickly will I see results?

+

Most users report noticing improvements within 2-4 weeks of consistent use. However, chronic pain management is a journey, and results vary by individual. The key is consistency with your personalized plan.

What makes Sail different from other pain apps?

+

Sail is built on rigorous scientific research in collaboration with leading pain researchers. We combine AI-powered personalization with evidence-based techniques from cognitive behavioral therapy, mindfulness, and physical therapy.
